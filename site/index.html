What we measured

    Why it matters here

    • Sinking ground has cut the Friant-Kern Canal's capacity by more than 60% along a 33-mile stretch, from about Pixley to the Kern County line. SJV Water
    • Design capacity 4,000 cubic feet per second versus about 1,600. U.S. Bureau of Reclamation
    • Repairing 10 miles (Phase 1) cost $326 million, as reported. SJV Water
    • The State Water Board may vote in April 2027 on a proposed pumping moratorium within two miles of the canal in southern Tulare County. It is a proposal and has not been adopted. SJV Water
    • California's Department of Water Resources publishes Sentinel-1 radar subsidence maps at 100 m, quarterly. That is the State map we compare with. data.gov

    Radar passes behind this map

    How it works

    1. Find every NISAR radar pair over the place in NASA's Alaska Satellite Facility catalogue.
    2. Read just the area needed from each radar product, with its own corrections and quality layers.
    3. Clean: remove the upper-atmosphere effect, drop cells where the signal is unreliable, take out leftover tilts.
    4. Stack the 12-day pairs into a time series with an uncertainty for every cell.
    5. Check against GPS stations and, where they exist, NASA's OPERA product and official maps.
    6. Publish dated map layers and a small data file that this page reads.

    What this can and cannot tell you

    • It reads too deep over cropland. In California, fields read about 4 cm lower than the town they surround, in 32 of 32 towns, and the extra signal follows the crop calendar. It is a crop-related radar effect, not confirmed sinking; the exact mechanism is not identified. We shade cropland there and do not count it. The same may apply to every place not yet checked.
    • Slanted view, not true vertical. The radar measures along its slanted line of sight. Vertical is roughly that divided by 0.77, with an assumed viewing angle.
    • Coarse cells, simple corrections. Cells are about 240 to 900 m. No weather model is used. San Joaquin comes from an earlier export built from preview pictures.
    • It over-reads at slowly moving stations.
    • Against California's State map.
    • It fails where motion is slow or the air is humid. We tried Houston and Jakarta: not detected.
    • Short record. Data for many regions begins in mid-June 2026.
    • Not for design or enforcement. Use it to decide where to look and whom to call, then confirm with surveyed data and official products.
